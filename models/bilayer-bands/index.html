Unfolding. Each eigenstate ψn of H(k) gets the weight Wn(k) = ⅓ Σℓ,s |Σα e−ik·rα cℓαs|² at structural momentum k. Layers and spins are summed incoherently. The weights of a state over k, k + K and k + K′ add to 1, and weights of degenerate states are shared equally. A(k, E) = Σn Wn(k) η² / ((E − En)² + η²), shown divided by 2 so that a doubly degenerate band with full weight reads 1.

Layer-resolved Sz. Every level is doubly degenerate, so the Sz of a single eigenvector depends on how the pair is chosen. The page plots the basis-independent trace over the degenerate pair, Sz,ℓ = Trpair[Pℓ σz], where Pℓ projects onto layer ℓ. Sz,lower = −Sz,upper for every pair (the total is zero), and Sz,ℓ(−k) = −Sz,ℓ(k). In the unfolded view the map shows Azℓ(k, μ) = Σn ⅓ (|aℓ↑|² − |aℓ↓|²) η² / ((μ − En)² + η²), with aℓs = Σα e−ik·rα cℓαs: the unfolded weight split by layer and spin.

Eigenvalues and eigenvectors come from Householder tridiagonalisation and implicit QL on the 24×24 real form of H(k). The folded Fermi surface contours a 150×150 grid over one magnetic BZ. The unfolded map interpolates energies and weights from a 120×120 grid and evaluates the Lorentzian at every pixel. Filling is electrons per Cr site (0–2, spin included).
